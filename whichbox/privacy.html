WhichBox

Privacy Policy

The short version: WhichBox doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your inventory stays on your device, and the developer never sees it.

What WhichBox keeps, and where

The places, rooms, boxes, items, notes, tags and photos you add are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. They aren't sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Like all app data, they're included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you've turned that on. Those backups are handled by Apple and by your settings, not by WhichBox.

Photos and the camera

WhichBox uses the camera or your photo library only when you add a photo, or when you scan a QR label. To help you find things, it reads the text printed on items in your photos and suggests tags and names. All of this happens on your device, using Apple's on-device features (including Apple Intelligence where available). Photos are never uploaded.

Siri, Spotlight and Shortcuts

So you can find things from your Home Screen, WhichBox adds the names, notes, printed text and box locations of your items to your device's Spotlight search. That index stays on your device. When you ask Siri to find something, Apple handles your request under Apple's Privacy Policy.

QR labels and NFC tags

A label or tag holds only a link with a random ID for the box, never what's inside. Someone who scans it sees a link that opens nothing unless they have that box in their own WhichBox.

Backup and Share Room files

When you choose Back Up or Share Room, WhichBox makes a file with your inventory (or one room), including photos. You decide where it goes: Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Messages or Mail. Anyone you give the file to can open it.

Deleting your data

Delete anything inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

WhichBox collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If WhichBox ever starts collecting any data, this page and the App Store listing will say so first.
